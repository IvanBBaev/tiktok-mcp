Acceptable use

The MIT License puts no restriction on what you may do with the code. These are the limits of what the project will build and support, and using it against them means you are on your own with both this project and TikTok.

The project uses official APIs only. There is no scraping, no reverse-engineered or undocumented endpoint, no watermark removal, and no attempt to obtain data TikTok does not expose to a developer application.
